# Ethos Technologies (`LIFE`) — Reproducible Calculations

> Draft calculation artifact for the final prospectus dated January 28, 2026 and filed January 30, 2026. Values are derived from the filing and are not price targets.

## Offering split

```text
Primary shares                     5,127,696
Secondary shares                   5,398,619
Total offered                     10,526,315

Secondary ratio = 5,398,619 / 10,526,315
                = 51.29%
```

Selling stockholders therefore offer slightly more shares than the company. Ethos receives no proceeds from the secondary component.

## Company proceeds

```text
Estimated net proceeds to Ethos      $82.6m
RSU tax withholding/remittance       $49.1m
Remaining broadly available capital  $33.5m

RSU-tax share of net proceeds = 49.1 / 82.6
                              = 59.44%
```

The remaining amount may be used for general corporate purposes, working capital, expenses, capital expenditures, or possible acquisitions without current material commitments.

## Implied equity and enterprise value

```text
IPO price                                  $19.00
Estimated fully diluted shares         68,339,479

Fully diluted equity value = 68,339,479 × $19
                            = $1,298.450m

Pro forma cash and investments            $198.315m
Commission-receivable financing liability  $27.813m

Estimated enterprise value = 1,298.450 + 27.813 - 198.315
                           = $1,127.948m
```

The commission-receivable financing liability is treated as debt-like for this illustration. A different classification would change enterprise value.

## Interpretation boundary

The calculations support a provisional valuation score, not a recommendation. A publication-ready report still requires an independent comparable-company set, external legal and regulatory review, conflict confirmation, and human verification of the filing extracts.
