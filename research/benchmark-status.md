## What the first comparison demonstrates

### ITG

- moderate operating growth;
- weakening profitability;
- leverage and debt-repair use of proceeds;
- sponsor and Up-C control;
- TRA, lock-up, and exchange complexity.

### Ethos

- rapid profitable growth;
- asset-light balance sheet;
- negative 2024 operating cash flow despite GAAP profitability;
- extreme carrier concentration and estimate-sensitive revenue;
- secondary selling exceeds primary issuance;
- 20-to-1 dual-class voting and venture-investor concentration;
- unresolved internal-control material weakness.

## Methodology lesson

The score should not rank a company only by revenue growth or profitability. Ethos is the stronger operating business, but receives only a modestly higher score because offering alignment, governance, concentration, cash conversion, dilution, and disclosure quality remain material.

## Next stress test

Generate Biomedicines will test whether the methodology can evaluate a pre-commercial issuer without assigning arbitrary zeros to revenue and profitability. The analysis must distinguish:

- genuinely not-applicable operating metrics;
- cash runway and research burn;
- human clinical evidence versus platform claims;
- partnership economics;
- probability-adjusted pipeline concentration;
- fully diluted financing risk.
